Questions? Answered 💬

Short answers to the questions we get most.

Who runs Green House Incentives?

Green House Incentives is run by a private U.S. company. We handle everything in-house: your application, verification of your receipt and efficiency rating, the decision, and the payment itself. There’s no office to visit and no intermediary in between.

Funding is limited and released in rounds, so claims are processed while the current funding pool is open. Applying early is the best way to make sure your claim lands in an active round.

ENERGY STAR® is a registered mark of the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.

How long does approval take?

Most claims are reviewed within 10 business days. If we need anything else — a clearer photo of your receipt, for example — we’ll email you.

Which purchases would qualify?

New (not second-hand) energy-efficient appliances bought from a U.S. retailer, with a valid receipt and a qualifying efficiency rating. See each category above for the exact bar.

How and when am I paid?

Choose direct deposit or a mailed check at approval. Payments go out within 4–6 weeks of the approval date.

Do I need to share bank logins or my SSN?

Never. We will never ask for bank passwords, card PINs, SSNs, or one-time codes. Anyone asking for those is attempting fraud.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. Applying is always free. Any site charging a “rebate release fee” is a scam.
